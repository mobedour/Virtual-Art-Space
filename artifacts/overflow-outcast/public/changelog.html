Virtual Art Space

First Beta Release

Amman Art Scene · Jordan · June 2026

An immersive virtual reality art exhibition platform — artists create and share 3D gallery rooms, visitors walk through them in the browser on any device. The platform has reached its first major milestone.

🏛️
v1.0.0-beta.1 — June 2026
Stages 1, 2, and 3 are complete. The platform ships with full auth, gallery & artwork management, an immersive 3D viewer with 6 room themes, live in-room edit mode, ambient audio, VR/WebXR headset support, room decorations, and desktop + mobile controls. Reviewing scope for the next version.
Pending before v1.0 stable: dedicated object storage for artwork images (replacing base64), custom domain acquisition, and next-version scope decision.

Stage 01
Core Platform
Complete

  • Artwork upload with full metadata
  • Profile editing & public gallery browsing
Stage 02
3D Gallery Viewer
Complete
  • Immersive first-person 3D room (React Three Fiber)
  • Desktop: WASD + pointer-lock mouse look
  • Mobile: touch-drag look + virtual joystick
  • Click / tap artwork to open detail modal
  • 5 room themes with lighting & fog presets
  • Spatial placement: wall / slot / height picker
Stage 03
Immersion & VR
Beta
  • Live in-room edit mode (drag, scale, rotate artworks)
  • Ambient audio per theme (Web Audio API)
  • 6th theme: Amman Limestone (terracotta tile, warm sandstone)
  • Room decorations — benches, plinths, plants, lamps
  • VR / WebXR headset support (Quest, Vision Pro)
  • Smooth movement, head bob, sprint, proximity glow
  • Object storage for artwork images (replaces base64)
Stage 04
Social & Scale
Planned
  • Social features — follows, likes, comments
  • Visitor analytics — heatmaps, engagement, dwell time
  • Multi-region CDN for 3D asset delivery
  • Asset optimisation pipeline (WebP, DRACO compression)
  • Multi-tenant support for galleries & institutions
