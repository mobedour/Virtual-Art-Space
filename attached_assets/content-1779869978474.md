AArt Art Season 2026 + 13th edition of 2026 AArt SHANGHAI will continue until JULY 15TH at TONGLELI PALACE, room 3-3.

Tongleli (同乐里) is a beautifully preserved 1905 historical block in the Jing'an District of Shanghai. Combining Queen Anne Revival and classic Shikumen architectural styles, it serves as a premier venue for exhibitions and cultural events, rather than a traditional palace. Built in 1905, it features grey brick arches, three-story colonnades, and ornate Corinthian columns. The property was once owned by the prominent Republican-era industrialist Yu Qiaqing and sits near the former residence of politician Tang Shaoyi.

AArt SHANGHAI, founded in 2012, is the first international hotel-based art fair in mainland China, having successfully held twelve editions to date. Its mission is to promote a “dialogue between art and life” through a display format that seamlessly integrates artworks into living spaces. AArt is also the only hotel art fair in Shanghai that focuses on comprehensive art consumption. With outstanding sales performance and high-quality original content planning, it has been selected as a support project for Shanghai’s cultural and creative industry and a major cultural event in Shanghai for many consecutive years.

十余年来，AArt汇聚大陆、港澳台、日本、韩国、东南亚等区域的画廊机构与艺术家，以国际视野打造独特的沪上品牌，成千上万的观众在 AArt 开启了自己的艺术收藏之旅；在新的十年之旅中，我们将持续致力于让更多家庭拥有艺术，让艺术家的灵感与表达围绕生活，推动艺术收藏成为一种触手可及的生活方式。
